Compound AI System Reliability: A Failure Taxonomy and Resilience Pattern Catalog from 150 Production Incidents

Researchers analyzed 150 real production incidents from compound AI systems (RAG, agents, orchestration) and built a taxonomy of 23 failure modes across five categories: retrieval, generation, tool, orchestration, and integration failures. Controlled fault-injection experiments show circuit breakers cut cascade propagation by 89%, output quality gates catch 73% of silent degradation, and systems using three or more resilience patterns cut mean-time-to-recovery by 71%.

Why it matters
This is close to a ready-made checklist for an AI Reliability role — you can cite specific patterns like circuit breakers and quality gates in interviews and directly map them onto your own test harness design for catching silent degradation before users do.

02 · arXiv cs.AI · 20 min read

trajectory-judge: What Outcome-Only LLM Judges Miss on Agent Trajectories

Using a deterministic fault-injection testbed, the paper shows outcome-only LLM judges can appear to detect trajectory faults while actually just flagging clean runs at similar rates, inflating apparent recall. It introduces 'paired discrimination'—flag rate on faults minus flag rate on matched clean runs—showing a 14B judge's real discrimination collapses to near zero on faults that don't change the final reply.

Why it matters
If you're building LLM-as-judge regression tests, paired discrimination against clean baselines is a technique worth adopting immediately to avoid shipping evals that look rigorous but aren't actually detecting anything.

03 · arXiv cs.AI · 20 min read

Dynamic LLM Routers are Often Misguided

Testing six commercial LLM routers across 14 settings and eight task categories, researchers found none beat a router that randomly picks between two well-chosen models at matched cost, with some underperforming by over 10 percentage points. They trace the gap to difficulty blindness, length reversal, semantic matching, and roster suboptimality.

Why it matters
If your reliability work touches cost/latency routing in a conversational pipeline, this gives you a concrete list of failure patterns to test for — and a reminder that routing claims need adversarial benchmarking, not vendor demos.
