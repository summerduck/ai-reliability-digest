03 · arXiv cs.AI · 35 min read

ReplayRCA: Measuring Post-hoc Diagnosis and Live Oversight of Agent Failures by Counterfactual Replay

ReplayRCA scores an agent's root-cause diagnosis by actually replaying the environment from the failure point with the proposed fix substituted in, rather than by agreement with human annotations. Across 13 frontier models on 62 failures, the top eight are statistically indistinguishable and the two largest vendors' flagship models rank 8th and 12th.

Why it matters
This intervention-based scoring method is a much stronger model than typical 'LLM-as-judge' evals for root-cause analysis — directly applicable if you're building regression/failure-analysis tooling for agent pipelines.

04 · arXiv cs.AI · 30 min read

One Verdict Is Not Enough: Regression Testing Learned World Models and Their Test Harnesses

TetraState runs each test under four model-native configurations and tracks per-test outcome transitions instead of trusting a single accuracy number, revealing that harness revisions moved aggregate accuracy by only 0.2-2.6 points while flipping 11-214 individual test labels, including regressions hidden behind an unchanged modal verdict.

Why it matters
This is a rigorous articulation of why single-metric regression checks fail for nondeterministic systems, and gives you a concrete pattern — configuration-level regression oracles — to reference when designing eval pipelines for LLM-powered products.

Trends this week

The harness is becoming a first-class testable artifact
The 'Portable Specification of the Agent Harness' paper, TetraState's harness-as-system-under-test protocol, Anthropic's explicit brain/hands separation, and LangChain's loop-engineering post all argue that the surrounding infrastructure, not just the model, must be versioned and tested.

Static, one-shot evals are being replaced by live or interventional testing
MIT Tech Review's call for real-time agent monitoring, ReplayRCA's counterfactual-replay scoring with a live-oversight track, and the CoT-controllability work on monitoring reasoning traces all push past pass/fail benchmarks toward continuous oversight.

Domain-specific, behavior-aware benchmarks are proliferating
o11y-bench (SRE/observability), DialFork (procedural hallucination in dialogue), and BULLPEN (behavioral model embeddings) each argue generic accuracy benchmarks miss the properties practitioners actually care about.
