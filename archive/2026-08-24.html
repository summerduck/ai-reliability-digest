05 · arXiv cs.AI · 30 min read

When Failures Propagate: Causal Failure Attribution in Agentic RAG

This paper introduces AgenticRAG-FP, a benchmark that injects certified faults at specific hops in multi-hop RAG trajectories and tests whether diagnosers can trace failures back to the true origin. Results show coverage-based diagnosis works well for immediate failures (0.91 at hop 1) but collapses entirely for propagated failures at later hops (0.00).

Why it matters
This is core to debugging real RAG pipelines — the finding that failures at hop 3 often originate at hop 1 (and vice versa with repair) is exactly the kind of root-cause analysis needed when triaging production RAG incidents.
