Anthropic shows that runtime infrastructure configuration alone—CPU, RAM, resource enforcement—can swing agentic coding benchmark scores (e.g., Terminal-Bench 2.0) by 6 percentage points, larger than typical leaderboard gaps between top models. Why it matters

05 · arXiv cs.AI · 25 min read

The Instability of Safety: How Random Seeds and Temperature Expose Inconsistent LLM Refusal Behavior

Testing four instruction-tuned models across 20 sampling configurations (4 temperatures x 5 seeds) on 876 harmful prompts, researchers find 18-28% of prompts show refusal 'decision flips' depending purely on seed/temperature. A Safety Stability Index shows stability drops significantly as temperature rises, challenging single-shot safety evaluation.

Why it matters
Directly validates the case for statistical/repeated-sampling regression testing of non-deterministic LLM behavior—cite this when arguing against single-run pass/fail gates in CI for conversational AI safety checks.
